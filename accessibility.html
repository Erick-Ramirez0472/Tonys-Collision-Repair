Accessibility

Last reviewed October 2026.

We want everyone to be able to use this site. It is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

What we've done

  • Text, buttons and form fields meet AA color contrast in both light and dark mode.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a skip link.
  • Form fields have visible labels, and errors are written out and announced to screen readers.
  • Pages reflow to one column on small screens and at 400% zoom, and zoom is never blocked.
  • Animation stops when your device is set to reduce motion, and the site supports high-contrast modes.

Known limitations

The quote form needs an email app on your device. If that doesn't work for you, call the shop.
