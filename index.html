Dealing with a claim?

Here is how a claim works when you bring your car to us.

You choose where your car is repaired.
Your insurer may suggest a shop, but the choice is yours. You can bring it to Tony's.
We write the estimate and work with your adjuster.
We document the damage, send the estimate, and handle the back-and-forth on the repair.
Have these ready when you call.
Your claim number, your adjuster's name and phone number, and photos of the damage if you have them.

How a repair works.

  1. Call or send photos

    Tell us what happened. Photos help us give you a faster answer.

  2. Get a written estimate

    We inspect the car and put the cost in writing before any work starts.

  3. We repair it

    Body, frame and paint work. We call if we find hidden damage.

  4. Pick it up

    Look it over with us before you drive away.
